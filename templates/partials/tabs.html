{# Keeps tab blocks that share a `group` in step: picking "template files" in one block picks it in every other block of the same group on the page. This is an enhancement, not the mechanism. The tabs themselves are a radio group in CSS (see sass/_components.scss), so without JavaScript every block still switches — just on its own rather than together. Panels keep their natural height, so a group switch can change the height of every block on the page at once — including ones above the block the visitor is looking at, which would otherwise shove their scroll position around. This measures the clicked block's position before and after the sync and scrolls by the difference in the same frame, so to the eye nothing above moves; only the tab the visitor clicked visibly changes. #}
