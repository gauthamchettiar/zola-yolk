Every listing on the site therefore has to ask the same question of a page before showing it, and a Tera macro returns a string rather than a list, so the question cannot be factored into one macro that hands back the pages that pass. It is instead written where each listing gathers its pages — here, in section.html, in the taxonomy templates, in macros/series.html, and in the atom.xml and sitemap.xml overrides. It reads the same in all of them: page.extra.list | default(value=config.extra.list | default(value="always")) A site-wide listing keeps only "always". A section's own listing, and a series, keep "local" too and drop only "never". #} {# Renders the

A list of paths is used as written — the author's own picks, in their own order. `true` finds them by tag instead: every post sharing at least one tag with this one, most tags in common first and, at equal footing, newest first. There is no way to attach a score to a page and sort on it — Tera has no map literal and cannot add a field to an existing object — so the ranking is done by making one pass per possible score, from every tag in common down to one. That is a handful of passes over a list of candidates, all of it at build time. Other parts of this post's own series are left out: they are already listed, in reading order, by the series panel at the top of the page. So are posts that keep themselves out of site-wide listings with `list` — but only when they are being found by tag.
